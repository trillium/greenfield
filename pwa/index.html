        How it works

        Say or type text here, then Submit. The helper on the chosen computer types it where the cursor is. A line break in the box is sent as the Enter key; the ↵ mark shows where.

        Only text is sent to a computer. Audio is never sent to it.

        What it cannot do

        • Listen in the background: dictation works only while this page is open and in front.
        • Be a share target, or react to sounds, or control windows or the pointer.
        • Use the microphone on a page that is not https (or localhost).

        Privacy

        Speech is recognised by your browser. On iPhone and iPad (Safari) and in Chrome, the browser may send the audio to Apple or Google to do it; this page cannot change that. Typing into the box sends nothing anywhere until you press Submit.

        The pairing token stays on this device and goes only to the computer it was made for. It lasts a day.
